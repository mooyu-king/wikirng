Privacy Policy

Your collection

Your browser saves each completed daily draw and its card data in local storage, including your draw time and quiz result for that draw. WikiRNG has no player accounts or server-backed collections. Clear this site’s browser data to remove local records. A separate browser profile has a separate collection. The installable site may cache its interface on your device.

Traffic measurement and Wikipedia requests

On wikirng.org, a traffic measurement script from data.1back.link records page visits for site statistics. Your daily cards, quiz choices and personal collection are not sent by the game to the traffic measurement endpoint. Your browser may contact Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons for card images. Those requests are subject to the Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy.

Sharing and support

The share action prepares the selected card, its image and links in your browser. You choose whether to save it, copy it or send it through your device. If you open a social platform, its own privacy policies apply. Messages you email us are used to respond to your request. Privacy questions can be sent to mooyuking@gmail.com.
