About Us

WikiRNG is an independent browser game for discovering English Wikipedia articles. Answer one quick question each day, reveal an illustrated card, and collect what you discover. You can browse the article pool between daily draws.

WikiRNG is independently made and has no affiliation with Wikipedia or the Wikimedia Foundation. Results describe a curated deck, and the game identifies the data and sources used in each edition.

Sources and licenses

The edition samples the English Wikipedia featured article index. The game uses daily counts from MediaWiki PageViewInfo to rank entries with a complete month of records.

Wikipedia contributors wrote the excerpts. WikiRNG trims them to fit the card and links to the source article and saved revision where available. The excerpts remain available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Media credits identify the author, original Wikimedia Commons file and its license. Article images may be resized or cropped to fit their frames. The game’s original cover and logo are AI-generated illustrations.

Install WikiRNG

Use your browser’s menu to install WikiRNG or add it to your home screen. On iPhone or iPad, open Share in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen.
